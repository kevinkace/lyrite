# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web app (the “Service”).

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Account Information:** When you sign in, we collect your email address and basic profile data provided by your authentication provider (Google, Facebook, etc.).
- **Usage Data:** We may collect anonymous analytics about how you use the Service (e.g., page visits, error logs).
- **Cookies:** We may use cookies or local storage to keep you signed in.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain your account  
- Improve and secure the Service  

## 3. Sharing Your Information

We only share your information with:

- **Supabase** (for authentication and database services)  
- Other service providers strictly necessary to operate the app  

We do **not** sell your data.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active.  
You can request deletion at any time via account settings.

## 5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and authentication to protect your data.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, update, or delete your personal data.

## 7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at [pii@lyrite.com](mailto:pii@lyrite.com).
